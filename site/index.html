The problem

A job passed through seven teams, each with its own logs. Nobody had the whole picture of where the time went.

Approach

From recorded interviews to a reviewed, evidence-backed map.

1

Interview by role

Recorded sessions and walkthroughs with each role, plus written follow-ups over chat.

2

Transcribe twice

Two speech-to-text models on every recording; where they disagreed, both readings went back to the owner.

3

Draw swimlanes

One page per process, lanes by department then job title, one action per box.

4

Review rounds

Manager comments tracked on the drawing with numbered tags and answered row by row.

5

Test against data

Pain points checked against job logs, fee records and time reports. Counts only, no client data copied.

6

Publish explorer

A generated website joins all pages, files and systems so anyone can follow a job across teams.

Where it goes next

Solution proposal

Proposal stage · Phase 1 next

Five root causes sit behind 9 problems.

No single job record

Staff link Excel files by hand.

Undefined hand-overs

Between the audit team and central support.

Deadline in the wrong place

The audit team owns it while the step waits on tax, head office or finance.

No fast route

A standard case takes the same route as a difficult one.

Tools don't connect

Built one at a time, they don't pass data on.

Suggested order of work

  1. Tax fee wait. Agreed turnaround plus an automatic reminder in the existing approval flow.
  2. Paper hand-overs. One cover sheet that travels with the pages. Report crossings between teams: 9 → 2.
  3. Double logs. Share the client ID across logs and read dates instead of re-keying. 8 manual steps removed.
  4. Payments. One route for payment slips and one payment report.
  5. Stamp duty. The request starts when the signed letter is saved.
  6. Pricing. A fee band the Audit Manager can approve: 8 steps → 3 for a standard fee. Awaiting a management decision.
  7. New-client hand-over and claims. Small fixes when the forms next change.
